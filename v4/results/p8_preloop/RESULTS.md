Two orthogonal tags per result (V4_README 6.1):
`error_class` = `exact` (zero error) or `approx` (charged to the error ledger);
`resource_class` = `unitary` (no extra qubits), `ancilla` (clean ancillas |0> -> |0>), or `measured`
(ancillas + mid-circuit measurement + feed-forward). A row is **hatched** iff
`resource_class != 'unitary'`. v3 had one word for both and hatched DPF Synth as relaxed on circuits
where it used ZERO ancillas.

## Caveats that travel with these numbers

* **DPF Synth is stochastic.** The seed range is in the table; it is 2 T at K=3 and 104 T at K=8 on qft_8 (9.8%). Any "we beat DPF" claim has to quote the range, not the best seed.
* **K\* is from our sweep**, not the paper's decision matrix, which is not in the public code.
* **DPF at K >= 4 is measurement-based.** We measured the ancilla leak: exact at k = 3 (state error 2.6e-16, zero leak), 1/sqrt(2) at k = 4, ~1.0 at k = 5. Those rows are `measured`; the 0-ancilla rows are `unitary`.
* **Certification.** `ours_certified` is False where the check was a proof for no application: above 2^14 basis inputs the exact check samples, and at 11-20 qubits a numeric check is a Haar-statevector lower bound. The algebraic certificate (V4_README 6.2) is a proof at any width and now covers most of the angle-circuit checks.
* **The gap to the best baseline is attributed** per circuit in `v4/SCOPE.md`, with a measured size for each mechanism, rather than left as a number.
