\begin{frame}{Why the editor formulation stalled}

{\footnotesize
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
\textbf{Three causes}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Hidden information.} Whether an angle can be fixed depends on a numerical
        re-fit the model cannot run --- it had to guess.
  \item \textbf{No reuse.} One accepted edit helps one window of one circuit; the next circuit
        starts from zero.
  \item \textbf{Unchecked lessons.} The reflector wrote prose that nothing verifies ---
        and one lesson \emph{banned the move that works}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
\textbf{Evidence (angle campaign, hhl\_8)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Lesson written: ``never snap unless within $10^{-4}$\,rad of $k\pi/4$''.
  \item Reality: fixes at $0.05$--$0.10$\,rad re-fitted to distance $\sim\!10^{-14}$,
        allowance $6.25\times10^{-5}$ --- accepted.
  \item 34 of $\sim$500 rotations ever visited; best arm $-147$\,T ($0.5\%$).
  \item BQSKit-DPF, the same idea applied to \emph{every} rotation: $35{,}450 \to 19{,}443$\,T.
\end{itemize}
\end{column}
\end{columns}}

\vspace{0.25cm}
{\footnotesize \alert{The gap was never knowledge --- DPF is a procedure the model can describe.
It was that a chat turn cannot execute one, and an edit cannot be reused.}}

\end{frame}
